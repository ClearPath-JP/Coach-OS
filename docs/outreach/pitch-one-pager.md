# Kindo — Founding Coach Pitch

---

## The problem every solo coach knows

You are a great coach. You are not a great admin system.

Right now your business probably lives across:
- A notebook (or your head) for session notes
- A spreadsheet for client tracking
- WhatsApp for check-ins and scheduling
- Venmo or Square for payments
- Maybe a Google Drive folder you barely open

Nothing talks to each other. You forget what you worked on last session. Clients slip through the cracks. You spend Sunday night catching up on paperwork instead of preparing to coach.

That is not a discipline problem. It is a tooling problem.

---

## What Kindo is

Kindo is a business operating system built specifically for solo martial arts and fitness conditioning coaches.

One place for everything:

- **Client tracking** — belts, benchmarks, conditioning scores, all in one profile per student
- **Session notes** — log what you covered, plan the next session before you leave the mat
- **Check-ins and homework** — assign work between sessions, see who actually does it
- **Scheduling and attendance** — your calendar, your clients, who showed and who did not
- **Group classes with pay-to-book** — set class slots, clients pay to secure their spot via Stripe before they show up
- **Dojo memberships** — sell recurring monthly plans to your students, automatic billing
- **Stripe billing and invoices** — get paid, send invoices, no more chasing
- **Coach-to-client messaging** — built-in, so it does not get buried in your texts
- **Branded client portal** — your students log in and see their own progress, homework, and schedule

No app juggling. No more "what did we work on last week?" No more unpaid invoices sitting in a text thread.

You are a solo martial arts or fitness coach. You work out of a garage gym, a park, a small studio, or online. You have somewhere between 5 and 30 clients. You are not running a big gym — you are running a tight, high-quality coaching practice, and you want the business side to match the level of your coaching.
