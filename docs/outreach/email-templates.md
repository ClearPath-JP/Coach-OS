# Email Templates — Founding Coach Outreach

Instructions: Send from a personal email, not a newsletter tool. Plain text reads more human than HTML. Keep subject lines lowercase — they feel like real emails, not blasts.

---

## Email 1 — Warm intro (someone who knows you)

**Subject:** quick question about the business side of your coaching

---

Hey [Name],

Hope [training / the gym / your clients] are going well.

I have been heads down building something I think you might actually find useful, and I wanted your honest take before it goes public.

You know as well as I do that the actual coaching is the easy part. The part that grinds you down is the admin — keeping track of where each client is, chasing payments, remembering what you covered last session, coordinating schedules across texts and spreadsheets.

I got tired of it and built a tool specifically for solo coaches: client tracking with belts and benchmarks, session notes with next-session planning built in, scheduling, group classes with pay-to-book, dojo memberships for recurring revenue, and Stripe billing all in one place. It is called Korva.

I am bringing on a small group of founding coaches at $99/month, locked in for life. Public pricing goes higher later. Founding coaches also get a direct line to influence what gets built.

If any part of that resonates, I would love to do a quick walkthrough — 15 minutes, I will show you exactly what it does, no obligation. If it is not a fit, I just want your honest feedback.

Either way, good to stay in touch.

[Your name]

## Email 2 — Cold outreach (coach you found via IG, a group, or a referral)

**Subject:** something I built for coaches running their own practice

---

Hey [Name],

I came across your work [on Instagram / through [mutual contact] / in [group name]] and wanted to reach out coach to coach.

I am [your name]. I coach [your style] out of [your setup] and I have been building a business tool specifically for solo martial arts and fitness coaches — the ones running 5 to 30 private clients out of a garage, a park, or a small studio.

The short version: it replaces the notebook, the spreadsheet, the WhatsApp group, and the Venmo requests with one place. Client profiles with belts and benchmarks, session notes with next-session planning, scheduling, group classes with pay-to-book, monthly membership plans, and built-in Stripe invoicing.

I am not pitching you — I know you did not ask for an email from a stranger. I am just building this for coaches who actually need it, and I want to make sure the right people know it exists before the founding price closes.

If you are curious, reply and I will send you a walkthrough. If not, no worries — either way I hope the coaching business is going well.

[Your name]

**Subject:** re: something I built for coaches

No pressure at all. I just wanted to make sure the timing piece was clear: the founding rate ($99/month locked in for life) closes once I have my first group of coaches set up. I do not have a hard deadline, but it will not be available indefinitely.

If now is not the right time, that is completely fine — you can always reach out later and I will do my best. I just did not want you to miss it if you were on the fence.

If you have 15 minutes and want to see what it does live, say the word.

[Your name]

---

## Notes on sending

- Always use your real name and reply from a real inbox. If they reply, respond the same day.
- Do not BCC multiple coaches on the same email. Each one should feel like it was written for them — because it should be.
- If you met them IRL, reference it in the first line. Context collapses the distance fast.
- For cold emails, if you can name something specific about their coaching (a class they posted, a belt they earned, a clip they shared), lead with that instead of the generic opener.
