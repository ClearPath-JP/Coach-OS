# Demo Script — Kindo Walkthrough

Two modes: the 60–90 second verbal pitch (for a DM call, a chance meeting, or opening a Zoom) and the 5-minute core-flow walkthrough (for a scheduled demo or when someone says "show me").

---

## Part 1 — The 60–90 Second Pitch (verbal, no screen)

Use this when someone asks "so what does it do?" in a conversation. Speak naturally — do not read it word for word.

---

**Say this:**

"So the problem it solves is the scattered mess most solo coaches are living in. You have got client notes in a notebook, schedules in texts, payments going through Venmo, maybe a spreadsheet you half-maintain. Nothing talks to each other and you are always missing something.

Kindo is one place for all of it. You open a client's profile and you can see their belt level, their conditioning benchmarks, the notes from every session, and exactly what you planned for next time. Before I had this, I would sometimes show up and ask 'so what were we working on?' — that is not a great look.

Beyond client tracking, it handles your scheduling and attendance, group classes where clients actually pay to book their spot before they show up, monthly membership plans for your recurring students, and Stripe billing so invoices go out automatically.

The whole thing is designed around how a solo coach actually works — not a big gym with staff, just one coach running a serious private practice.

I am bringing on a small group of founding coaches at $99 a month, locked in for life. Want me to show you what it actually looks like?"

---

**If they say yes:** move to Part 2.
**If they say "maybe, how long does it take?" say:** "Fifteen minutes. I will just show you the core flow. If it is not for you after that, I will tell you myself."

---

## Part 2 — 5-Minute Core Flow Walkthrough (screen share)

Set up before the call: log into coach.foundos.ai as the demo coach. Have one real-looking test client already in the system with a few session notes so the demo feels lived-in, not empty.

---

### Stop 1 — Dashboard (30 seconds)

**Click:** Dashboard

**Say:**
"This is what you see when you log in. At a glance — your upcoming sessions, any client check-ins waiting, recent activity. The point is not to overwhelm you with data, it is to surface the things that need your attention today."

---

### Stop 2 — Client profile (90 seconds)

**Click:** Clients → open the test client

**Say:**
"Here is a client profile. You can see their belt level or conditioning tier, their benchmarks — whatever metrics you actually track for this person — and the full session history below. Every time you log a session, it stays here.

Watch this — I will open the last session note."

**Click:** the most recent session note

**Say:**
"This is what I logged after our last session. What we worked on, how they did, and — this is the part that changed how I coach — I write the plan for the next session right here, before I close it. So when I show up next week I already know exactly where we left off and what we are building toward. No more starting the session trying to remember."

---

### Stop 3 — Schedule and group class (60 seconds)

**Click:** Schedule (or Classes)

**Say:**
"Here is the schedule. I can see all my upcoming sessions and which clients are booked in. For coaches running group classes — say you have a Saturday morning conditioning class — you set the slot here with a capacity limit, and clients pay to secure their spot before they show up. That is it. No more people saying they are coming and then not showing. They paid — they show."

---

### Stop 4 — Get paid (60 seconds)

**Click:** Invoices (or Billing / Payments)

**Say:**
"And here is the billing side. You can send invoices, see what is paid, what is outstanding. If you run dojo memberships — monthly plans — those bill automatically through Stripe. You are not chasing anyone. The money just comes in on the same day every month.

For most coaches, that alone is worth it. The time they spend following up on payments is real, and it is awkward. This takes it off your plate."

---

### Stop 5 — Client portal (30 seconds)

**Say:**
"One more thing — your clients get their own login. They can see their progress, their upcoming sessions, their homework. It makes the relationship feel more professional without you having to do anything extra. It is just there."

---

### Close (30 seconds)

**Say:**
"That is the core of it. Client tracking, session notes with forward planning, scheduling, group classes with pay-to-book, memberships, and billing — all in one place.

The founding rate is $99 a month, locked in for life. I am only doing that for the first group of coaches, and once those spots are filled, public pricing is higher.

You can try it free and see how it feels in your actual practice. If after a week it has not made your life simpler, you cancel and owe nothing. But I think once you have your clients in there and you have logged a few sessions, you will not want to go back to the old way.

Want to open your dojo?"

---

## Notes

- Do not rush the client profile section. That is where it clicks for most coaches.
- If they have a specific pain point they mentioned earlier (chasing payments, forgetting session details, no-shows to classes), steer the walkthrough toward that stop first.
- If the demo coach asks a question mid-walkthrough, answer it honestly. Do not stay on script if it kills the conversation.
- Have coach.foundos.ai open before the call. An empty account looks like nothing — populate it with a few test clients and real-looking notes.
