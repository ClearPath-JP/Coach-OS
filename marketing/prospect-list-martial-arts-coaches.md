## NOTES FOR OUTREACH

- **Do NOT lead with features.** Lead with their specific pain point (see "Outreach hook" for each).
- **Reference something specific** about their business to show you actually looked at their profile.
- **The pitch:** "I noticed you are managing [X] across [platforms]. We built COACH-OS specifically for coaches like you -- one place for clients, scheduling, payments, messaging, video content, and programs."
- **Best channels:** Instagram DM for smaller coaches, email via website contact forms for coaches with websites.
- **Timing:** Many of these coaches post daily -- engage with their content for a few days before reaching out.
